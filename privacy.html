应用信息

应用名称: QR Code-Barcode Generator（二维码与条形码生成器）

开发者: Vladimir Gavranic

适用版本: 1.0.0 及以上

数据收集与使用

本应用根据您自己输入的内容（文字、链接、Wi-Fi 信息、联系人信息、电子邮件、电话号码、短信或条形码数字）在您的设备上生成二维码和条形码。所有内容仅在您的设备上处理，本应用不会上传、发送或共享。本应用不联网，没有账户，不使用统计分析或广告，不使用相机。我们不会收集、传输或与任何人共享您的数据。

应用会在设备上的“My codes”列表中记录您保存或分享过的代码，其中可能包含您输入的内容（例如 Wi-Fi 密码或电话号码），并保存上次使用的设置（纠错等级、尺寸、颜色）。这些数据仅保存在您的设备上，本应用已关闭云备份。

权限说明

本应用不申请任何权限，包括网络权限、存储权限和相机权限。应用将生成的代码图片保存到手机的 Pictures/QRBarcode 文件夹。

分享

如果您点击“Share”（分享），代码图片将交给您自己选择的应用（例如电子邮件或即时通讯），去向完全由您决定，开发者不会收到任何内容。

数据主体权利

您可以随时在应用内删除“My codes”中的单条记录或清空整个列表。已保存的图片位于您的相册中，您可以自行删除。卸载应用将删除列表和设置；已保存的图片会保留，直到您自行删除。

免责声明

打印或发布之前，请用扫码应用测试生成的代码。本应用不负责商品注册：用于商店销售的 EAN-13、EAN-8 和 UPC-A 编码须向 GS1 申请。Wi-Fi 二维码以明文包含网络密码，任何看到该图片的人都可以读取密码。

联系方式

如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请通过 gavranic76@gmail.com 联系我们。

QR Code-Barcode Generator - Privacy Policy (English)

App Information

App name: QR Code-Barcode Generator

Developer: Vladimir Gavranic

Applies to: version 1.0.0 and later

Data Collection and Usage

The app creates QR codes and barcodes on your device from the content that you type yourself (text, links, Wi-Fi details, contact details, e-mail, phone numbers, SMS or barcode digits). Everything is processed only on your device; the app does not upload, send or share it. The app does not use the internet and has no accounts, analytics or advertising, and it does not use the camera. We do not collect, transmit or share your data with anyone.

The app keeps a list called "My codes" on the device with the codes you saved or shared. It can include what you typed (for example a Wi-Fi password or a phone number). It also keeps its last settings (error correction, size, colour). This data stays on your device only, and cloud backup is switched off for this app.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions, including no network, storage or camera permission. It saves the code pictures into your phone's Pictures/QRBarcode folder.

Sharing

If you tap "Share", the code picture is handed to the app you choose (for example e-mail or a messenger). Where it goes is entirely your decision; the developer receives nothing.

Your Rights

You can delete single entries or the whole "My codes" list in the app at any time. The saved pictures are in your gallery and you can delete them yourself. Uninstalling the app removes the list and the settings; the saved pictures stay until you delete them.

Disclaimer

Test every code with a scanner app before you print or publish it. The app does not register products: EAN-13, EAN-8 and UPC-A numbers used to sell goods in shops are issued by GS1. Wi-Fi codes contain the network password in plain form, so anyone who sees the picture can read it.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at gavranic76@gmail.com.
